## Interpretation

The fusion model improves F1 and average precision over the structured baseline in this generated cohort. It does not outperform every ablation: removing retrieval yields higher average precision, and removing class weighting improves calibration. Temperature scaling does not reduce the primary model's holdout ECE. These outcomes are reported without assuming that each paper component must improve performance.

Empirical conformal label-set coverage: **88.9%**, nominal target 90%; mean set size 1.422; ambiguous-set abstention rate 42.2%. The chronological setting does not establish the exchangeability assumptions needed for a coverage guarantee.
